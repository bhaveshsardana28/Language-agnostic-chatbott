Palette, typography & motion rationale

Ink, paper, a human accent

Paper #F4F2ED, canvas #FCFBF8, ink #292E2A, vermilion #B7412B, sage #E5E9DD. Fine rules, 3–4 px corners and restrained paper shadows.

An editorial voice

Georgia display and reading accents, paired with the system UI sans. The language index makes scripts feel like a considered collection of words. System Indic fallbacks; no external font requests.

Motion: a page turning

Proposed: 180–240 ms fades and 6 px entrances; a 380 ms language-marker handoff. The voice seal would respond to real mic levels. This study stays static; reduced motion removes hover transitions.
