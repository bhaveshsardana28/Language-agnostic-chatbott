PALETTE, TYPOGRAPHY & MOTION RATIONALE

One unmistakable signal

Graphite #111510, panel #171C15, bone #F0F2E8, chartreuse #DAFA68, sage-grey #A1A997. Square geometry, thin technical rules, deliberate accent light around voice states.

Language as the graphic

Helvetica/Arial display and UI paired with SF Mono/Consolas metadata. Large Latin/Indic glyph compositions create the identity. No stock robot, sparkle, purple gradient or glass layer.

Motion: a change in signal

Proposed: decisive 160–220 ms transitions; a 300 ms language handoff; a 420 ms panel settle. Voice geometry would scale with actual mic amplitude. This study is static and reduced-motion friendly.
