Palette, typography & motion rationale

A warm place to land

Cream #FFFDF7, cocoa #352C25, forest #365848, mint #E6EEE1, peach #F8DFCE, clay #A43F28. Soft 16–24 px corners, nested paper cards and shallow tactile shadows.

Friendly, never childish

Avenir Next (Trebuchet/system fallback) for UI and headlines, Georgia italic for human emphasis. Language postcards are the brand illustration. All imagery is inline SVG/CSS; no external assets.

Motion: gently conversational

Proposed: 220–280 ms soft-settling entrances, 2–4 px press/hover feedback, a 420 ms postcard greeting handoff. Voice ripples would use real levels. Static here; reduced motion removes the hover lift.
